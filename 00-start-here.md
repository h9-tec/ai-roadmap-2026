# Start here

## Who this is for

This roadmap is for people who can already program and want to become AI engineers: people who build products on top of existing models, not people who train models from scratch. You might be a student, a backend or web developer, or a data analyst moving into AI work.

## Prerequisites

- You can write small programs in any programming language. Layer 1 brings you up to speed on Python.
- You can read technical English. Most docs, papers, and courses are in English.
- You have about 10 hours a week. Every layer's time budget assumes this.

## How to study

Study project-first:

1. Skim the layer's resources so you know what is in them.
2. Build the layer's mini-project.
3. When you get stuck, go back to the resources for the part you need.

Keep a public repo of your mini-projects. Each one builds on the last, and together they show employers what you can do.

## 12-week sample plan

This plan is a compressed, near full-time path. It moves at roughly double the pace of the layer time budgets (about 25 weeks at ~10 hrs/week), so plan on about 20 hours a week.

Layer 4 (Evaluation) comes before Layer 3 here, so every later project ships with evals.

| Week | Layer | Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | 1. Foundations | LLM proxy API |
| 3-5 | 2. LLM Engineering | Docs Q&A with citations |
| 6-7 | 4. Evaluation | Eval suite for the docs Q&A |
| 8-9 | 3. Agents | Research agent with a sandbox |
| 10-12 | 5. Production (LLMOps) | Research agent deployed with guardrails |
